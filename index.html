Getting started

  1. Open this page on your Android device and download the APK.
  2. Open the downloaded file and follow Android's installation prompts. If prompted, allow installation from the browser or file manager you used.
  3. Launch Vacation Scheduler and create your local login. Choose a passphrase of 15 to 128 characters, then start adding your vacations.

The application runs on your device, not in your browser. Printing and email sharing use compatible services or apps installed on your device.

Your records are stored locally. Clearing app storage or uninstalling deletes local data. There is no password recovery. Share exported reports only with people you trust.
